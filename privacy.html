Privacy policy

Alfred Kisisel is a private application with exactly one user: its owner. No one else can sign in.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail (read and draft), Google Calendar events, and one Google Drive folder (read only).
  • Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own server to help him plan his day. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Sending and changes: the app never sends mail or changes the calendar without the owner's explicit approval each time. It cannot delete mail or edit Drive files.
  • Revoking access: the owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
